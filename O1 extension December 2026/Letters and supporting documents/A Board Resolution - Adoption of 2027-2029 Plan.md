# OCTET INC.
## ACTION BY UNANIMOUS WRITTEN CONSENT OF THE BOARD OF DIRECTORS

[December 1, 2026]

The undersigned, being all of the members of the Board of Directors (the "Board") of Octet Inc., a Delaware corporation (the "Company"), take the following actions by unanimous written consent without a meeting, under Section 141(f) of the Delaware General Corporation Law and the Company's Bylaws. They direct that this consent be filed with the minutes of the proceedings of the Board.

### Recitals

A. Amal Devani has served as the Company's Chief Executive Officer since September 2022. The Company employs him in that position under O-1A classification, most recently under Petition IOE8795688197, approved by U.S. Citizenship and Immigration Services ("USCIS") on October 13, 2026, with validity through December 13, 2026.

B. Between 2023 and 2026, under Mr. Devani's leadership, the Company completed the development phase of its business:
- obtained New York State Liquor Authority Warehouse Permit No. 0577-24-03109;
- built a marketplace that keeps every transaction within the regulated three-tier system;
- established merchant partnerships with New York fine wine retailers;
- integrated with the OpenTable and Resy reservation systems; and
- rebuilt the Grapevine platform during 2026, adding the virtual cellar and an AI system that writes wine tasting notes at catalogue scale.

C. The Board has reviewed a plan, presented by Mr. Devani, for the commercial launch and scale-up of the rebuilt Grapevine platform from December 14, 2026 through December 13, 2029 (the "2027–2029 Plan"), summarised in Schedule 1.

### Resolutions

**1. Adoption of the 2027–2029 Plan.** RESOLVED, that the Board adopts the 2027–2029 Plan as the Company's operating plan for the period December 14, 2026 through December 13, 2029.

**2. Continued employment of the Chief Executive Officer.** RESOLVED, that the Company shall continue to employ Amal Devani as its Founder and Chief Executive Officer, full time, for the period December 14, 2026 through December 13, 2029, to lead the execution of the 2027–2029 Plan, with the duties he has held since September 2022:
- directing the Company's product and technical build;
- negotiating and executing its commercial agreements;
- managing its regulatory filings and licensing;
- leading all financing; and
- reporting to the Board.

**3. Compensation.** RESOLVED, that Mr. Devani's annual base salary shall remain $180,000. The Executive Compensation Deferral Agreement dated April 24, 2025, approved by the Board that day, remains in full force and effect. [ALTERNATIVE, if payroll has resumed: …and that the Company shall pay that salary through its regular payroll with effect from [date], any amounts previously deferred remaining payable in accordance with that Agreement.]

**4. Immigration filings.** RESOLVED, that the Company is authorised to file with USCIS a Form I-129 petition, with premium processing, to extend Mr. Devani's O-1A classification and stay for the period December 14, 2026 through December 13, 2029, together with all related forms, fees and supporting documents. Danny O'Farrell, Director, is authorised to sign the petition and supporting letters on the Company's behalf.

**5. General authority.** RESOLVED, that the officers and directors of the Company are authorised to take all further actions and execute all documents they consider necessary or appropriate to carry out the foregoing resolutions.

This consent may be executed in counterparts and by electronic signature. Each counterpart is an original, and together they form one instrument.
